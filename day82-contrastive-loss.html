DLFROMZERO · DAY 82

Contrastive loss — a collapsed encoder scores a perfect 1.0 on alignment, and the loss number means nothing across batch sizes.

The batch is part of the loss, not a training detail

NT-Xent asks each view to pick its partner out of a line-up, and the line-up is the rest of the batch. So the batch size changes the objective, not just the gradient noise.

Temperature is a negative-mining knob

Everyone describes tau as sharpening the softmax. True, but the useful reading is about where the gradient goes: the share landing on the single hardest negative, against everything else in the batch combined.

The degenerate solution scores perfectly on half the objective

Collapse means every embedding is the same point. Alignment — the average cosine between the two views of an item — is then a perfect 1.0. Only uniformity notices anything is wrong.

The loss, with the numerics that keep it finite

At tau = 0.01 a cosine of 1 becomes a logit of 100, and exp(100) is fine but exp(1000) is Infinity. A naive log(sum(exp(.))) returns NaN, which is not a large loss, it is no loss at all.
